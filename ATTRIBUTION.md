## Launcher icon

The vintage CRT icon in `app/icon-retro-tv.png` and `app/largeIcon-retro-tv.png`, plus the older equivalent `icon.png` and `largeIcon.png`, was generated for this launcher using OpenAI's built-in ImageGen. The source is `docs/app-icon.png`. It is separate from the original site's TV artwork.

Generation prompt: A polished square retro CRT television icon with walnut cabinet, cream bezel, two tuning knobs, rabbit-ear antennas and colourful broadcast test bars, on a dark forest-green background; clean silhouette and safe margins; no text or watermark.
